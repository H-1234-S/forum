## Why

The AI programming community needs baseline safety controls so publish-first user-generated content can remain usable without adding pre-publication review. Rate limiting, sensitive-word replacement, and image validation reduce obvious abuse in v1.

## What Changes

- Add rate limiting requirements for high-risk actions.
- Add sensitive-word replacement requirements for articles and comments.
- Add image validation requirements for avatars and article images.

## Impact

- Affected capability: `safety-and-governance`
- Supports publishing, commenting, reporting, avatar upload, article image upload, and moderation workflows.
