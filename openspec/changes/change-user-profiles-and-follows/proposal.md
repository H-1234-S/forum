## Why

The AI programming community needs public profiles, personal centers, and follow relationships so users can present their technical identity, discover authors, and build social signals for future recommendations.

## What Changes

- Add public profile pages for users.
- Add authenticated personal center entry points.
- Add profile editing for avatar, nickname, and bio.
- Add follow and unfollow behavior.
- Add favorites page for collected articles.

## Impact

- Affected capability: `user-profiles-and-follows`
- Supports social discovery, author pages, personalization signals, and personal content management.
