## Why

The AI programming community needs article comments and interaction actions so readers can discuss technical content, signal quality, collect useful articles, and share public article links.

## What Changes

- Add two-level article comments.
- Add author edit and delete behavior for comments and replies.
- Add article like and unlike behavior.
- Add article collect and uncollect behavior.
- Add article sharing behavior and share signals when applicable.

## Impact

- Affected capability: `comments-and-interactions`
- Supports article discussion, engagement counts, recommendation signals, notifications, and personal favorites.
